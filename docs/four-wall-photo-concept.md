# Four wall photos and a future 3D kitchen preview

Four still photos can support a visual concept, but cannot guarantee a scale-accurate room: perspective, hidden corners and lens distortion can make measurements ambiguous. Do not label inferred geometry as measured or use it to price or order cabinetry. No image intake or 3D pipeline is enabled in this change.

A first usable version would generate the 3D room from the same verified walls, ceiling, openings, appliance positions and cabinet SKU dimensions as the 2D planner. Four optional wall-labeled photos could later suggest openings and room features alongside user-entered measurements. Require at least wall lengths, ceiling height, a known scale reference and manual validation. Use editable suggestions for uncertain features, never silently replace measurements. For more automatic capture, investigate overlapping photos and device depth separately and show where accuracy is unverified.

Before enabling photo intake, define user consent, storage/retention and deletion, restrict access, and test on rooms with occlusions, wide-angle lenses and irregular geometry. Keep the plan preliminary until the customer or a professional remeasures before fabrication.
